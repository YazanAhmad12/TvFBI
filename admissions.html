Admissions

Maya Academy is open to all women and girls who are motivated to learn sewing and fashion design the process is simple and flexible students can apply directly by contacting the academy choosing the course that matches their goals and registering for the upcoming session our admission philosophy is to make education accessible and supportive so that every student feels welcome regardless of her background or level of experience the goal is to give every woman the chance to join a community of learning creativity and empowerment

Start Your Training Journey

Start your academic journey at Maya Academy by taking the first step toward learning a skill that can transform your life here every course is designed to guide you from the basics to advanced levels with hands on practice and continuous support your journey begins the moment you decide to learn and it grows with every project you complete and every new skill you master this journey is not only about sewing it is about building confidence discovering creativity and opening doors to new opportunities in your personal and professional life
