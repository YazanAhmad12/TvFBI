Our graduates are women who completed their training with confidence skill and creativity many of them started small businesses from home others continued into professional fashion careers and all of them carry the spirit of empowerment and success from Maya Academy into their personal and professional lives.

Our Alumni Legacy

The alumni of Maya Academy carry a legacy of creativity independence and empowerment their work and achievements reflect the values of the academy many graduates have built small businesses others became professional designers and many continue to inspire their communities the legacy they leave is proof that the skills and confidence gained at the academy live on and create lasting impact beyond the classroom

Our Graduates

Success Stories

Sara

Sara joined Maya Academy in 2019 with no background in sewing after completing the home sewing course she started creating clothes for her family and friends today she runs a small online store where she sells handmade dresses and household items and she is proud to be financially independent

Omar

Omar joined the academy in 2020 with an interest in learning practical skills he mastered machine sewing and tailoring during his course after graduation he started working in a local tailoring shop where he applies his skills every day and supports his family with a stable income

Amani

Amani enrolled in the fashion design pathway in 2021 her passion for creativity grew with every project she completed after graduating she was invited to present her designs in a local university event and now she dreams of building her own fashion brand that reflects her style and cultural identity

Creating Lasting Impact

Maya Academy creates lasting impact by empowering women with skills, confidence, and creativity. Our programs go beyond teaching sewing and fashion design—they inspire students to apply their knowledge in real projects. Many graduates start small businesses, become community leaders, or share their skills with others, ensuring the positive effects of learning continue long after the course ends.
